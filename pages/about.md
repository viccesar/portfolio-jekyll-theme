## About Me

My name is Dr Victor Vargas. I am based in Berlin, Germany, and I have a multidisciplinary background in engineering, business administration, education, coaching, and psychology.

## Education and Professional Background

My academic and professional experience spans engineering, project management, higher education, and applied behavioural sciences. I currently work as a Senior Lecturer and Programme Leader, with experience in curriculum design, research supervision, and academic leadership.

## Industry and Interests

My main interests include data analytics, machine learning, innovation management, human-centred design, project management, and mental health in organisations. I am particularly interested in how data-driven approaches can support better decision-making.

## Recent Projects

One of my recent projects focused on predicting taxi booking cancellations using machine learning. The project involved feature engineering, model comparison, hyperparameter tuning, and lift analysis to support operational decision-making.

## Goals

My goal is to further develop practical data analytics skills and apply them to real-world challenges in business and education. I aim to bridge technical analysis with human and organisational impact.

## Personal Interests

Outside of work, I enjoy continuous learning, exploring different cultures, and spending time with my West Highland Terrier.
